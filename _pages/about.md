I am pursuing a PhD in the Aeronautics and Astronautics Department at Stanford Univeristy, where I am advised by Professor Mykel Kochenderfer in the Stanford Intelligent Systems Laboratory. My current research is focused on creating new algorithms for validation of safety critical autonomous systems. My research interests also include planning, reinforcement learning, and optimization. In 2020 I received a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from Georgia Tech and in 2022 I received my M.S. in Aeronautics and Astronautics from Stanford. I am a recipient of the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship,
